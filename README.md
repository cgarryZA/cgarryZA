## Christian Garry

Stochastic analysis and numerical methods: McKean–Vlasov forward–backward SDEs with jumps, their
well-posedness, a posteriori error estimates for their solvers, and Lean 4 formalisations of the
results. MEng Electronic Engineering and MSc Scientific Computing, Durham University.

**Research**

- *Well-posedness of fully coupled McKean–Vlasov FBSDEs with jumps under full-tuple law
  dependence*, with C. Feng. [arXiv:2608.23203](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23203), with its
  Lean formalisation in
  [mvfbsdej-well-posedness-lean](https://github.com/cgarryZA/mvfbsdej-well-posedness-lean).
- [LevyStochCalc](https://github.com/cgarryZA/LevyStochCalc): a Lean 4 + Mathlib library of
  Lévy-driven stochastic calculus (Itô and Poisson integrals, the Itô–Lévy formula, BSDEs with
  jumps).

**Tools**

- [MachineChecked](https://github.com/cgarryZA/MachineChecked): read a mathematical paper beside
  its Lean formalisation, claim by claim. Site: [machinechecked.org](https://machinechecked.org).
- [papercheck](https://github.com/cgarryZA/papercheck): a reproducible audit harness for
  mathematical LaTeX papers.
- [Ariadne](https://github.com/cgarryZA/Ariadne): an MCP server for running a literature review.

**Hardware**

- [deepJFET](https://github.com/cgarryZA/deepJFET): an Intel 4004 built from silicon-carbide JFET
  logic and simulated at transistor level.
- [advent-of-camel-2025](https://github.com/cgarryZA/advent-of-camel-2025): Advent of Code 2025
  as cycle-accurate FPGA designs in Hardcaml.
